# Phase 6b manual test: color, type, Now Playing, lists, Download (Oppo A1k, Android 9)

Install `app/build/outputs/apk/debug/app-debug.apk`. Android 9 has no dynamic color, so everything below uses the teal fallback palette. Mark each ✅/❌. Nothing here has been seen on a device yet.

To switch theme on Android 9: Developer options → Night mode → Always on / Always off, then reopen Sur.

1. **Light theme**: Playlists, Songs, Download, the mini player and Now Playing all look like layered surfaces (cards are visibly lighter or darker than the background), titles are large and bold, and secondary text is clearly greyer than titles. No purple anywhere. ☐
2. **Dark theme**: repeat 1 in dark. Text is readable on every surface, and the tab bar, cards and mini player are distinguishable from the background. ☐
3. **Now Playing, portrait**: on a song with colorful art, the top of the screen is tinted toward the art's color, and the seek bar, play button and active shuffle/repeat use that color. Title, artist and times stay readable. At 360x640-size screens, nothing overlaps or is cut off. ☐
4. **Now Playing, color follows the art**: skip between songs with different art. The tint and accent change with a short fade (no flash, no stutter) and the artwork crossfades. On a song with no art (or grey art), colors fall back to the teal theme. ☐
5. **Now Playing, landscape and controls**: rotate while playing. Artwork is on the left and controls on the right, nothing overflows, and the Up Next card is hidden. Back in portrait, tap Up Next and the queue opens, highlighting the current song. Tap a queue song to jump to it. Play/pause animates its icon, prev/next work, and next is greyed out on the last song with repeat off. ☐
6. **Songs scroll (130 songs)**: each row shows a rounded thumbnail (or note placeholder), title, artist · duration. Fling quickly top to bottom and back several times. There's no visible stutter, thumbnails fill in shortly after rows appear, and there's no crash or out-of-memory error. The currently playing song shows in teal with a small level-bars icon, and the mark moves when the song changes. ☐
7. **Playlists mosaic and empty state**: a playlist with 4+ songs that have art shows a 2x2 cover. 2–3 songs show plain tiles in the empty slots. 1 song shows its art full size. An empty playlist shows the amber icon tile. Counts match the real number of songs. With no playlists at all, "Create playlist" opens the name dialog and creates one. No drag handles or fake play buttons on cards. ☐
8. **Mini player**: shows a thumbnail, title, artist and a thin teal progress line that advances. Play/pause works (the icon fades), next works and is disabled when there's no next song, and tapping the bar opens Now Playing. ☐
9. **Download full flow**: in the YouTube app, Share a video → Sur. The Download tab opens with the link filled in. Tap Download and watch the steps: "Resolving…" with Cancel, then a card with the song title, artist and a moving progress bar with Cancel, then "Saving…", then the success card. Tap **Play** and the downloaded song plays. Then try a bad link and check the error card shows a clear message, and that **Try again** and **Dismiss** work. Check Cancel mid-download too. ☐
10. **Outdoors and memory**: use Sur in bright daylight and check titles, secondary text and the seek bar are readable. Then use the app normally for 10 minutes (switch tabs, open Now Playing, scroll, skip songs). It stays responsive with no growing lag, and nothing gets killed or restarts. ☐
