# Phase 9c manual test: Find info single-song UI (Oppo A1k, Android 9)

Install `app/build/outputs/apk/debug/app-debug.apk`. Verify the single-song Find info lookup workflow, candidate selection, artwork preview, and override application/reset. Mark each ✅/❌.

1. **Consent appears once and Cancel does nothing**: on a fresh install or clear data, tap overflow (⋮) on any song row and choose "Find info". Verify the consent dialog appears with the explanation text. Tap Cancel: verify the dialog dismisses and no lookup runs. Tap "Find info" again, tap Allow: verify the Find info sheet opens and subsequent taps on "Find info" open the sheet directly without asking for consent again. ☐
2. **Search a downloaded song that has no cover**: open Find info for a downloaded song lacking artwork. Verify the title and artist fields are pre-filled from QueryCleaner variants. Tap "Search". Verify a progress indicator shows while querying MusicBrainz and ranked candidate results appear. ☐
3. **Edit the title text and re-search**: in the Find info sheet, manually edit the Title field (or tap "Try another query" to cycle clean variants) and tap "Search". Verify new search results are fetched and displayed according to the edited query. ☐
4. **Pick a candidate and see its cover preview**: tap on a candidate card in the results list. Verify the candidate highlights as selected and cover art begins fetching. Verify the cover art preview displays (or "No cover found" if unavailable), while the Apply button remains enabled. ☐
5. **Apply, then check the cover and title in the Songs list, Mini player and Now Playing**: tap Apply with a candidate and cover selected. Verify the sheet closes, and the song's updated title and cover art immediately appear in the Songs list. Start playing the song: verify the new title and artwork display in the Mini player and Now Playing screen. (Note: a song that is already playing keeps its old info until it is queued again.) ☐
6. **Remove found info**: open "Find info" for the song that now has an applied override. Verify the "Remove found info" button is visible. Tap "Remove found info": verify the override is removed, the cover file is deleted, and the Songs list reverts to the original MediaStore tags and artwork. ☐
7. **Airplane mode shows a plain error**: turn on Airplane Mode (disconnect from network). Open "Find info" and tap "Search". Verify a plain-language error message ("Couldn't reach the service") is displayed without crashing. ☐
8. **Skip**: open "Find info" for an unmodified song and tap "Skip". Verify the song is marked skipped in the database and the sheet dismisses cleanly. ☐
9. **Android 13+ phone**: verify the Find info workflow, consent, cover preview, and overlay updates on an Android 13+ device. ☐
